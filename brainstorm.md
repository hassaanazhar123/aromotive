# Aromotive design exploration

| Theme | Overview | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| Liquid Gold Garage | An editorial luxury-detailing environment, built around obsidian surfaces, warm metallic highlights, and reflective product plinths. It makes car care feel like a ritual rather than a chore. | 0.07 |
| Karachi Night Drive | Urban motion, petrol-station light, wet asphalt, and dense street-sign typography evoke a Pakistani night drive. It is energetic and practical but risks overpowering the product range. | 0.04 |
| The Scent Archive | A fragrance-house catalogue translated to automotive care, using specimen labels and tonal paper. It prioritizes perfume but underplays cleaners and accessories. | 0.08 |

## Selected direction

**Liquid Gold Garage** is the chosen direction because Aromotive already signals a premium fragrance-led car-care brand through its gold automotive logotype. The visual system positions the full catalogue—from perfume to microfiber—as a coherent premium maintenance ritual.
