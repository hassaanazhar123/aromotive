# Aromotive design contract

## Design movement and reference

**Liquid Gold Garage** interprets luxury automotive detailing through the material language of precision workshop tools and private fragrance houses. It is not a generic black-and-gold storefront: wide, asymmetrical product stages and machined label details take their form from car body panels, bottle caps, and detailing bays.

## Core principles

1. **Reflective restraint:** Near-black fields make gold details feel intentional; empty space is used like a product plinth.
2. **Ritual clarity:** Shopping routes follow the real care sequence—scent, wash, cabin, wheels, finish, tools.
3. **Tactile precision:** Fine rules, condensed utility labels, and polished surface textures reference workshop instruments rather than decorative luxury.
4. **Operational calm:** The CRM is light, legible, data-first, and uses the same metallic accent only for an action that changes customer-facing content.

## Brand essence

**Aromotive is Pakistan's premium car-care ritual: lasting cabin fragrance and exacting detailing products for drivers who maintain every journey.**

Personality: **assured, sensorial, precise**.

## Voice

Headlines are compact and sensory; supporting copy is direct, specific, and never overclaims. Calls to action use active verbs.

- “Make the cabin memorable.”
- “Build your wash ritual.”

Avoid generic phrases such as “Welcome to our website,” “Get started,” and “best quality.”

## Color philosophy

## Typography system

`Cormorant Garamond` (display) brings fragrance-house drama to heading lines. `Manrope` (body) keeps shopping and administration clear. `Barlow Condensed` (utility) is used in uppercase labels, prices, and product codes. Display headings are 52–88 px desktop / 38–56 px mobile, weighted 500–600; body is 14–17 px at 1.6 line-height; utility text is 11–13 px with generous tracking.

## Wordmark and logo

The supplied Aromotive black-and-gold car-and-scent logo is the primary mark. It is placed in a controlled dark frame in the shop header and used sparingly in the CRM lock-up.

## Signature elements

- A **gold oil-line**: a thin horizontal rule that expands on interaction and separates meaningful content.
- **Product aperture cards**: large image wells framed by a narrow, offset metallic corner.
- **Ritual index**: small category codes (`SCENT 01`, `WASH 02`, etc.) that locate products within the vehicle-care sequence.

## Brand visual assets

Photography is required because product cleaning results and cabin atmosphere depend on material credibility. Art direction favors dark, high-contrast automotive surfaces, wet gloss, warm reflected gold, and products photographed against clean studio shadows. Hero imagery stays text-safe on the left; cards use controlled, consistent crops. The supplied logo remains the only exact brand mark.

## Layout paradigm

The storefront uses a **roadside strip** rather than a conventional centered grid: an anchor rail holds navigation and editorial labels, while product content moves in broad, offset lanes.

```text
[utility rail]  [mark]                          [bag]
[gold line]    [hero thesis / action]  [vehicle surface image]
[ritual code]  [category / product lane]    [sliding product cards]
[proof rail]   [journal or delivery section]
```

## Interaction philosophy

Every interaction is grounded in shopping intent. Cards reveal a concise benefit and add action; quantity and cart actions respond immediately; admin publishing tasks visibly change state. Keyboard focus uses gold outlines, and motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Animation

On page load, the logo settles before the hero content rises by 12 px; the gold oil-line expands horizontally once. Product cards use a restrained 180 ms lift and image-scale transition. No looping ambient animation is used.

## Self-review

The initial direction could have become a generic dark luxury treatment. To make it specific to Aromotive, the interface uses the supplied automotive fragrance mark as a functional anchor; its category sequence mirrors an actual vehicle-detailing routine, and its offset lanes mimic a car-panel silhouette rather than an interchangeable card grid.
